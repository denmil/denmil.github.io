Publications

Selected Papers

Peer-reviewed research from my PhD in medical computer vision.

IF 7.5 2022

Automated Estimation of Chronological Age from Panoramic Dental X-ray Images Using Deep Learning

Expert Systems with Applications

Deep learning approach for automated age estimation from panoramic dental X-rays. Median error of 2.95 years for adults — outperforming established manual methods, fully automated, and non-destructive.

86K+ samples 2022

A Comprehensive Exploration of Neural Networks for Forensic Analysis of Adult Single Tooth X-ray Images

IEEE Access

Forensic dental analysis using 86,495 annotated teeth — one of the largest datasets in the literature. Automated models match or beat manual forensic methods for age, sex, and tooth identification.
